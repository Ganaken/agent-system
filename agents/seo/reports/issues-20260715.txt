Mulak.app — Top SEO & Trust Issues to Fix (2026-07-15)
Plain English, most important first. WHAT is wrong and WHY it matters.

1. Your Privacy Policy and Terms pages still literally say "placeholder text pending final legal review." Anyone who reads them — a customer, Google, or an AI assistant — sees unfinished legal pages on a service that handles people's rent and cheque money. That wrecks trust instantly and could be a legal problem.

2. You advertise "bank-grade" security, but there's no page anywhere that backs it up — the "Security" link just scrolls to a marketing section. Making a big safety promise with zero proof scares off serious customers and can look misleading.

3. When someone mistypes your web address or clicks an old link, they get silently dumped on your locked login screen instead of a normal "page not found." Google sees this as broken behavior and it can quietly drag down how your whole site ranks.

4. Your site is really just one thin sales page (about 1,000 words total — no blog, guides, or FAQ). There's almost nothing for Google or AI tools like ChatGPT to read, so you simply won't appear when Dubai landlords search for help or answers.

5. The competitors sitting on page one of Google have lots of pages (features, comparisons, "best software" lists, UAE-rules guides). Google rarely ranks a single one-pager for competitive searches like "Dubai property management software," so today you're basically invisible for your most valuable keywords.

6. Your homepage is slower than it should be because it loads 17 font files and a big off-screen image before the main picture even appears. Slow pages annoy visitors and Google counts speed against you in rankings.

7. Your Arabic version only works as an on-screen button — Google can't see or list it. So every landlord searching in Arabic finds nothing from you, even though Arabic speakers are a core part of your market.

8. Several links in your footer are fake or point to the wrong place: "Blog" and "Security" just jump around the homepage, and "About" doesn't go to your real About page. This frustrates visitors and wastes Google's time hitting dead ends.

9. You have no customer reviews, testimonials, or listings on trusted sites like G2 or Capterra, and your social icons link to blank Twitter/LinkedIn pages. With no outside proof your company exists, buyers and AI tools have little reason to trust or recommend you.

10. Your Google title shows your brand name twice ("Mulak — Property Management for Dubai — Mulak"), and the pricing/company details hidden in your page's code don't match what's shown on screen. These are small, sloppy signals that make Google trust and understand your business less.
